aggregation methodni back to ToC or Named Individual ToC

IRI: https://w3id.org/fair-o#AggregationMethod

A FAIR-O aggregation method describes how sub-principle or principle scores are combined into aggregate FAIR scores.

calculation algorithmni back to ToC or Named Individual ToC

IRI: https://w3id.org/fair-o#CalculationAlgorithm

A FAIR-O calculation algorithm is aligned with FTR scoring algorithms, but FAIR-O uses it specifically to describe how a sub-principle result is computed.

f a i r assessmentni back to ToC or Named Individual ToC

IRI: https://w3id.org/fair-o#FAIRAssessment

A FAIR-O assessment groups interpreted FAIR assessment outcomes for one assessed digital object. It can be related to an FTR result set when test-level execution details are available.

sub principle resultni back to ToC or Named Individual ToC

IRI: https://w3id.org/fair-o#SubPrincipleResult

A FAIR-O sub-principle result may be derived from one or more FTR test results, but it is normalized at the FAIR sub-principle level rather than at the individual test level.
